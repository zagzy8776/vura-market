Vura Privacy Policy

Vura collects account, contact, delivery and transaction information needed to provide marketplace services, process payments, deliver orders, prevent abuse and support customers.

Information we use

We may process your name, email, phone number, delivery address, order history, payment references and technical information needed to secure your session.

Payments

Payment details are handled by our payment provider. Vura does not store your full card details.

Data choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information where applicable. Contact Vura support through the contact details published on the site.

Updates

We may update this policy as the service changes. The latest version will always be published here.
