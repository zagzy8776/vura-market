Vura Terms of Service

Vura is a curated commerce service. Product availability, condition, specifications and delivery details are shown as accurately as possible and are confirmed during order fulfilment.

Orders

An order becomes confirmed after successful payment and availability confirmation. If an item cannot be sourced, Vura will communicate the next step and applicable refund process.

Returns and complaints

Customers should report product or delivery problems promptly. Vura will review complaints against the product description, condition and applicable return policy.

Prices

The price shown at checkout is the customer-facing retail price. Internal supplier costs and Vura margins are confidential business information.

Contact

Vura will publish current support contact information on the storefront as the business launches.
